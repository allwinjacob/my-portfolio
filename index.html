About

Who I Am

I'm an Embedded Systems and R&D Engineer with over two years of experience building embedded products and electronic systems from the ground up. Currently serving as Team Lead at Evolve Robotics LLP, I work across the full spectrum of hardware engineering — from circuit design and PCB layout to firmware and system integration.

Alongside my professional work, I'm pursuing an M.Tech in VLSI & Embedded Systems, deepening my expertise in RTL design, digital logic, and processor architecture. I recently designed a complete 32-bit RV32I RISC-V processor in Verilog — achieving zero timing violations after synthesis.

I'm driven by the challenge of building reliable, efficient systems at the intersection of hardware and software — whether optimising firmware on an STM32 or exploring clock gating techniques in FPGA designs.

VLSI & RTL Design
Designing digital logic and processor architectures in Verilog/SystemVerilog, with synthesis and timing analysis in AMD Vivado.
Embedded Firmware
Writing reliable firmware in C/C++ for STM32, ESP32, PIC and Raspberry Pi across industrial and IoT applications.
Electronic System Design
Designing schematics and PCBs using Eagle, integrating sensors and communication interfaces — SPI, I2C, UART and CAN.
